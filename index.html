Privacy & scope notice

All PDF parsing, text detection, OCR, redaction and file generation in this tool run locally, inside your browser tab, on your own device. The document you open is never uploaded to a server, sent to any cloud storage, or transmitted to any AI or third-party service by this tool. Nothing about the document or the values it contains is written to disk, logged, or retained after you close or reload this page.

This tool is intended to help with data minimisation before sharing a document with an external party or AI system, and to support your office's confidentiality practices. It does not, by itself, guarantee compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000, applicable ICAI confidentiality requirements, client engagement terms, or any other law, rule, or professional obligation. Detection is pattern-based and best-effort: it can miss items it was not configured to find, misclassify content, or over- or under-detect in scanned or unusually formatted documents. You remain responsible for reviewing every match before relying on the redacted output.

What "permanent" means here

When you finalize a document, each page is flattened into a fresh image with the chosen areas painted opaque black before the page is rasterised — not a black box placed on top of live text. The output PDF is then rebuilt from these images only, so the original text layer, hidden/invisible text, any existing OCR text layer, embedded attachments, form fields, annotations and document metadata are not carried over into the new file. The trade-off is that the exported PDF is image-based rather than text-searchable.

What this tool cannot promise

  • That every instance of sensitive data in the document has been found — especially free-text addresses, signatures, unlabelled ID numbers, or unusual formats.
  • That content you did not select for redaction is safe to share.
  • Legal, regulatory or professional-standards compliance of any kind.

How this tool works

1. Open a document

Drag a PDF in, or choose one from disk. Pages render as you view them; thumbnails appear in the Pages tab.

2. Choose what to detect

Tick the categories of sensitive data relevant to this document, or use "Select all sensitive data". Add your own terms or a custom regular expression under "Other". Enable OCR if the PDF contains scanned pages with no selectable text.

3. Scan, review, and mark manual boxes

"Scan document" highlights every match on the page it appears on. Click a highlight (or an entry in "Review matches") to include or exclude it. Switch on "Manual redaction mode" to draw your own boxes over signatures, logos, table cells, or anything a pattern can't catch — drag to draw, drag the handles to resize, drag the body to move, and use the × button to delete.

4. Redact and finalize

"Redact selected" or "Redact all detected" burns the chosen areas into the page images. "Finalize, verify & download" rebuilds the whole PDF from flattened images, runs a verification pass that tries to extract text back out of the result, and gives you the file to download along with an audit summary.
